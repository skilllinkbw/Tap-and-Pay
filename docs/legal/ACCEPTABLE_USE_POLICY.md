# Tap & Pay — Acceptable Use Policy

**Product:** Tap & Pay, a Braincade Holdings Pty Ltd product
**Status:** DRAFT — pending final legal review.

This policy defines prohibited activity on the Tap & Pay platform. Breach may
result in suspension/termination and reporting to payment partners or law
enforcement.

## Prohibited activity

1. **Fraud** — accepting payments for goods/services that do not exist,
   processing fictitious transactions ("transaction laundering"), or using the
   Service to defraud customers, banks, or payment providers.
2. **Money laundering / terrorist financing** — structuring, layering, or any
   use designed to disguise the origin of funds, or any activity contrary to
   applicable AML/CFT law.
3. **Unauthorised transactions** — charging a customer without their
   authorisation, inflating amounts after authorisation, or forcing a
   transaction through after a decline.
4. **Credential theft** — harvesting OTPs, session tokens, card data or other
   credentials; attempting to capture full card numbers, CVVs or PINs
   (the app is deliberately built so this is not possible — do not attempt to
   modify it to do so).
5. **Payment manipulation** — tampering with amounts, references, QR payloads,
   NFC interactions, or transaction records; replaying captured payment data.
6. **Malicious activity** — reverse engineering for attack purposes, injecting
   malware, interfering with the app, its backend or its providers, or probing
   systems without written authorisation.
7. **Abuse of the platform** — denial-of-service, automated abuse, credential
   stuffing, or exceeding documented API limits.
8. **Unauthorised access** — using another merchant's account, device or
   terminal, or exceeding your assigned role (owner/manager/cashier).
9. **Circumvention of security controls** — disabling device integrity checks,
   running the Service on compromised devices in production, or bypassing
   idempotency/duplicate-payment protections.
10. **Illegal trade** — using the Service for goods or services that are
    unlawful in Botswana or the customer's jurisdiction, or that violate
    card-scheme / mobile-money rules.

## Enforcement

Suspected violations are investigated using the platform's audit trail
(correlation IDs, transaction references, security events). We may suspend
access pending investigation and will report where legally required.

## Reporting

Report abuse or security concerns via the in-app *Settings → Help* channel, or
per the [Incident Response Plan](../security/INCIDENT_RESPONSE_PLAN.md).
